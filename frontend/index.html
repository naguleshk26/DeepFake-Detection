Multi-Layered Verification Engine

AI Deepfake Detection

PyTorch neural networks analyze spatial facial characteristics, GAN blending boundaries, and synthetic texture artifacts across image and video crops.

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Performs differential JPEG quantization re-compression to highlight localized compression anomalies, spliced regions, and photo modifications.

EXIF & Device Forensics

Extracts camera hardware metadata, lens attributes, timestamp chains, and software signatures (Photoshop, Canva, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion).

Video Frame & Face Tracking

Extracts video keyframes at optimal intervals, isolates detected faces using OpenCV, and tracks frame-by-frame deepfake probability variations.
