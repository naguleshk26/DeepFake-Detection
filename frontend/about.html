DeepGuard System Methodology

Understanding AI deepfakes, digital image forensics, and signal fusion architecture.

What Are Deepfakes & Synthetic Media?

Deepfakes are synthetic digital media (images, videos, or audio clips) created or altered using generative deep learning algorithms such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Diffusion Models (e.g. Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, DALL-E), and autoencoders (e.g. DeepFaceLab).

These algorithms learn facial structures, lighting transitions, and skin textures to swap faces or generate realistic non-existent human countenances. While visual quality can appear convincing to the human eye, AI generators leave mathematical artifacts in frequency spectra, pixel noise distributions, and compression grids.

Multi-Layered Forensic Signals

1. Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Re-saves images under controlled 95% JPEG quantization. Original camera photos exhibit uniform degradation, whereas edited or composite areas display elevated error levels due to multi-compression history.

2. Noise & Frequency Artifacts

Divides image into spatial patches to measure sensor noise standard deviation variance. Performs 2D Discrete Fourier Transforms (FFT) to detect periodic grid spikes produced by GAN upsampling filters.

3. EXIF & Software Signatures

Inspects embedded EXIF headers for camera hardware (Make, Model, Lens), creation timestamps, and software modification signatures (Photoshop, Canva, Midjourney, LightX).

4. PyTorch Deep Learning Model

Employs a PyTorch Convolutional Neural Network trained on deepfake face datasets to evaluate facial symmetry, eye alignment, and boundary inconsistencies.

Classification Thresholds & Scoring

DeepGuard combines individual signal outputs into a unified manipulation score. Classification boundaries are centrally managed in config.py:

0% – 30%

LIKELY AUTHENTIC

Low manipulation indicators across forensic signals.

31% – 70%

SUSPICIOUS

Inconsistent or mixed forensic signal readings.

71% – 100%

LIKELY AI-GENERATED

High synthetic or deepfake manipulation probability.

System Disclaimer & Limitations: Media verification algorithms are probabilistic tools. Heavily compressed social media files (e.g. WhatsApp, Instagram) may strip EXIF metadata or introduce compression artifacts that mimic manipulation. DeepGuard provides AI-based prediction insights, not 100% legal proof.
